# Reflection 5 in Module 5

### Key Take-Away & Advice
- **Key Take-Away:** I learned why database normalization is important and how 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF help reduce duplicate data and prevent update, insert, and delete anomalies. I also learned that denormalization can improve read performance but comes with extra costs for updates and storage.
- **Advice:** Understand why tables are split during normalization instead of just memorising the different normal forms.

### What Was Good?
- I found creating `normalize.db` and generating approximately 200,000 rows in a flat table interesting. It was useful to work with a larger dataset and see how the database could be organised into smaller related tables.

### What Was Difficult & Where Did I Struggle?
- Understanding functional dependencies and how to decide which attributes belong in separate tables required some thought. Comparing normalized and denormalized designs also helped me understand that there is no single best design for every situation.
