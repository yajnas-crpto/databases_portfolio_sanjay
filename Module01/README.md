## Task What is IoT

Domains and included areas:
- Industrial IoT (IIoT) & Industry 4.0: Smart manufacturing, CNC and machine tool monitoring, predictive maintenance, and digital twins.
- Smart Home & Building Automation: Environmental sensing, automated lighting, smart HVAC control, and energy management systems.
- Smart Cities & Infrastructure: Intelligent traffic management, smart grids, automated waste collection, and environmental monitoring.
- Healthcare & Medical IoT: Remote patient telemetry, smart medical equipment, and wearable health-tracking devices.

Commonly used (data) protocols:
- MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport): A lightweight publish-subscribe protocol designed for bandwidth-constrained, high-latency networks and resource-limited edge devices.
- HTTP/HTTPS & REST APIs: Standard web protocols used for device configuration, management dashboards, and cloud integration.
- CoAP (Constrained Application Protocol): A specialized web transfer protocol tailored for constrained nodes and low-power IoT networks.
- Modbus / OPC UA: Industrial communication protocols widely used to interface with PLCs, sensors, and factory automation hardware.

Typical devices (appliance or micro controller):
- Microcontrollers & Single-Board Computers: ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and STM32 development boards.
- Industrial Hardware: Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs), smart industrial sensors, CNC milling machines, and 3D printers.
- Smart Consumer Appliances: Connected thermostats, automated lighting systems, IP security cameras, and wearable fitness trackers.

Challenges:
- Network Reliability: Handling intermittent connectivity, offline periods, and unstable network conditions without losing critical diagnostic data.
- Data Storage & Management: Efficiently storing, querying, and filtering massive streams of high-frequency time-series telemetry data locally on edge devices or in cloud repositories.
- Security & Privacy: Securing resource-constrained nodes against vulnerabilities, unauthorized access, and man-in-the-middle attacks.
- Power & Resource Constraints: Operating under strict CPU, memory, and battery limitations on embedded hardware.
